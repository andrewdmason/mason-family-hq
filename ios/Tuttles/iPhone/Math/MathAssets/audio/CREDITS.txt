Math runner audio: sources and licences
=======================================

Every file here is either public domain (CC0 1.0) material from Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
or was synthesised from scratch by scripts/math-audio/build.py, or a mix of the two.
The synthesised sounds are original work made for this app and are likewise released
as CC0 1.0. No attribution is required for any of it.

Kenney packs used (each pack's own License.txt says Creative Commons Zero, CC0):
  kenney-music-loops       Music Loops (1.1)
  kenney-impact-sounds     Impact Sounds (1.0)
  kenney-interface-sounds  Interface Sounds (1.0)
  http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Rebuild with: python3 scripts/math-audio/build.py <jam-folder>
