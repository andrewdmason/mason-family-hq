Math runner models: sources and licences
========================================

Every model here is from a pack by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released as public
domain (Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0). No attribution is required. The files
are unchanged; the game recolours and resizes them when it loads them.

  characters/   Mini Characters (1.0): the twelve people, their swatch sheet,
                and two pairs of glasses
  furniture/    Furniture Kit: desk, table, lounge chair, large fridge,
                computer screen, trash can, potted plant
  city/         City Kit (Suburban): two trees
  food/         Food Kit: what's on the cafeteria tables

They were fetched from the copies in github.com/series-ai/jam-ready-assets,
which keeps each pack's own License.txt beside it.

Everything else in the game (the school, what stands in the way, what the
teacher throws) is built in code: MathProps.swift and MathZones.swift.

The house (Tuttles/iPhone/House) uses more of the same, fetched from the same
place:

  furniture/    Furniture Kit: the rest of what's here, some eighty pieces
  pets/         Cube Pets (1.0): the twenty-four animals and their swatch sheet

The house's rooms (Tuttles/iPhone/House) also use, all public domain
(CC0 1.0) too:

  house3d/      Ultimate House Interior Pack (June 2020) by Quaternius
                (quaternius.com): 123 pieces, converted from the pack's .obj
                files by prototypes/tuttles-house-3d/obj2glb.py. Fetched from
                the author's copy at opengameart.org/node/126986. Used for
                inside doors and for the pieces HouseKit.better names.
  ../house3d/   Floor, wood, plaster, cloth, stone and grass textures from
                Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), resized to 1024 pixels.
